What is
SUAS?

The Student Unmanned Aerial Systems competition is a demanding autonomous-aircraft challenge. Teams design, integrate, document, and demonstrate a complete UAS that can safely execute a realistic mission.

Why it matters

A real systems-engineering challenge.

SUAS brings together aircraft design, autonomy, remote sensing, safety, documentation, and field operations. It is exactly the kind of long-horizon goal that gives our fixed-wing work a meaningful direction. Our first priority is building the foundations; the published 2027 mission and rules may change, so we will work from the official handbook as it is released.

01

Design

Engineer an integrated vehicle and ground system.

02

Navigate and act on a mission with onboard systems.

03

04

Demonstration

Execute the mission safely in the field.
