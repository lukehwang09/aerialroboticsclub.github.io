Engineering made visible

Our middle-school outreach makes UAV engineering tangible: an aircraft to see, a team to ask questions, and an inviting first look at what students can build.

What a visit can include

A real aircraft.
A real invitation.

We keep visits practical and interactive. Instead of a long presentation, we use the work in front of us to show how a student team takes an idea through design, testing, and flight.

01 / See

Aircraft
walkthrough

Meet a fixed-wing aircraft up close and trace the systems that make it fly: structure, propulsion, electronics, and control.

02 / Try

Hands-on
challenge

Work through a short engineering prompt designed to reward questions, iteration, and creative problem-solving.

03 / Ask

Team
conversation

Hear honestly from students about starting without experience, learning technical skills, and working through setbacks.
