Three ways to get involved

Every build needs all three — most members end up trying each of them by second semester.

01

Airframe

CAD, 3D-printed mounts, frame assembly, and repairs. Hands-on work with real tools from day one.

02

Electronics & software

Wiring, flight controller setup, and the code behind autonomous missions. Python and a bit of C++.

03

Test & ops

Flight testing, safety checks, and getting a build competition-ready. You keep the team honest about what's airworthy.
